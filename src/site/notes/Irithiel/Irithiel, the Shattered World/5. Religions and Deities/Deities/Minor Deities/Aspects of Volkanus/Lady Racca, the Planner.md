## Origins:

To aid with the reconstruction of [[Thracia\|Thracia]] after Volkanus' [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Ascent to Godhood\|ascent]], he removed his sense of proportion- and from it crafted Lady Racca. 


## Activity:

Lady Racca operates independently from [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Volkanus]] and serves as one of his advisors for his projects; when he chooses to listen to her. When dealing with mortals, she will often take the form of a dwarven master mason, architect or planner. She gives excellent advice, and is secretly the mind behind many of the architectural marvels of the world, thought most of them have been lost to time.
