Anuket is a Minor God.

###### Regional Names: 
She Who Feeds the Sea
Achelus ([[Thracia\|Thracia]])
###### Domains: 
Nature, Tempest

###### Regional Worship: 
Not commonly worshiped, but with isolated shrines in river-bound fishing villages or small towns prone to flooding.

## Nature:

Anuket, ironically given her role, is best described as "Still waters run deep". She will be calm and steady until the moment she flips; and either becomes very friendly or very hostile. 

Her role is to bring people, material and ideas to her Master, [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Thalassus of the Oceans\|Thalassus]]. This very functional role keeps her busy most of the time.

## Origins:

When the [[Bassaria\|Bassarian]] culture was at its height, and the [[King Apsu-Toli\|King Apsu-Toli]] was most venerated by his people, some very strange things began to happen. 

The first of these strange events was the movement of the waters. Snow-capped mountains began to shed their white covering; ice beginning to melt for the very first time, and the water began to collect in lowlands. As this started to flood and overwhelm the residents of his country, King Apsu-Toli instructed his third minister, Karda Basmir, to fix the situation. He had no way of knowing this, but he had just decided on the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Ascent to Godhood\|ascent]] of the first mortal to become a Minor God.

After heading out, Karda experienced the world's first river; she began to bend its course using magic and labour, trying to avoid the houses and farms that she could. This proved impossible at the time, and many drowned. Karda's grief as she was overtaken by the waters can still be seen in her unyielding service to her former King. 


## Activity:

Anuket brings everything she can to the realm of her master, which keeps her busy most of the time. To aid her, she invites natural spirits to occupy each river and stream, which has resulted in the [[Naiad\|Naiad]] population we see today. 

Anuket herself works mostly as an administrator, echoing her former mortal life, hearing the petitions of her Naiads as mortals affect the rivers. 

In recent times, Anuket suffered greatly in the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/2. Others/4. Cimmeria/Fall of Cimmeria\|Fall of Cimmeria]], being grievously wounded by the impact- collateral damage rather than an intentional target. [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Thalassus of the Oceans\|Thalassus]] looks to restore her, so she now recuperates in the bay that resulted by the fall, unknowingly entrapping [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Other Minor Deities/Jaraxx, the Rot\|Jaraxx, the Rot]]. 

Her absence since then has resulted in more river-work industry among the mortals, and a decided increase in hostility in the [[Naiad\|Naiad]].
