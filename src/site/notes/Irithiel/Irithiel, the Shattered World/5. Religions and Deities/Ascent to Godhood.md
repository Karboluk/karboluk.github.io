The Ascent to Godhood can happen in many ways- it's unknown how this happened to the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Elder Gods\|Elder Gods]] as it predates history, and even [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Helios the Radiant Truth\|Helios]]'s ascension can only be speculated at. 
The gods can simply incarnate another, as happened with [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Helios/Parvati, the Accepting\|Parvati]] and the other muses, or elevate lower beings like [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Maahes/Kor, the Tireless\|Kor]], [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Astere/Trikar, the Shrike\|Trikar]] and [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Astere/Frouros, the Watchman\|Frouros]]; but a few more unique scenarios are thought to be known:

- [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Thalassus of the Oceans\|Thalassus]], the God-Emperor of his people, is said to have ascended by the faith empowering him. Other stories contradict this, specifically the much more recent ascension of Zatar.
	- The Ascent of Thalassus caused the [[Irithiel/ZZZ. Private DM Info/49. Secrets/Events and Activities/Sundering of the Lands\|Sundering of the Lands]]
- [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Maahes of Shield and Blade\|Maahes]], a hero, conquered a foe so overpowering that the effort required to do so necessitated deific power. His ascent could be attributed to his valour, or to how much the people of [[Thracia\|Thracia]] needed him to achieve victory.
	- The Ascent of Maahes destroyed [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Maahes/Apophis, the Worthy Foe\|Apophis]], and his ongoing presence shattered Thracia. 
- [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Volkanus]] ascended after [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Maahes of Shield and Blade\|Maahes]]' presence had eroded the land of Thracia into an archipelago; his ascension was likely not from mortal origins given the relative lack of destruction. 
	- A Volcano was added, some ocean filled in, a previously endless war stopped for years. 
- [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Zatar the Ravaging Storm\|Zatar]] ascended when she learned the method that [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Thalassus of the Oceans\|Thalassus]] used to ascend. 
	- The Ascent of Zatar resulted in the end of the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/2. Others/4. Cimmeria/Fall of Cimmeria\|Fall Of Cimmeria]].
