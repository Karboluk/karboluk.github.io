# The Pantheon


<div class="transclusion internal-embed is-loaded"><div class="markdown-embed">



## Context
Dwarves on Irithiel are in a unique position, in that they were purpose-built by one of the gods, [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Moradin]], and were done so within historical memory; but also because the dwarven gods physically spend time with their children, helping and teaching, or having the dwarves assist them in their own great undertakings.

Many of the members of this pantheon are known by other names, to other peoples, but the dwarves know that their relationship with the gods, and hence the names they use, is correct.

### Domains
Clerical Domains are described, but some dwarves apply the teachings of their chosen god outside of these areas- they are not prescriptive.

### Piety Benefits for Adherents
A grown dwarf following one of their gods has likely spent some time in that god's presence, and some of the god's divine power will begin to reside in them. Each deity listed below conveys a unique benefit, though some dwarves show other boons.

# Major Gods

### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Moradin]]
Symbol: A Hammer and Anvil
Represents: Leadership, Crafting
Cleric Domains: Life, Light, Forge


The father of the dwarves crafted his children from metal and gems and imbued them with souls as he cooled them with his breath.

Moradin is the master of every craft practiced by the dwarves and the patron of artisans. He expects his children to follow in his footsteps, studying his techniques and aspiring to one day match his expertise.

Priests of Moradin are responsible for judging and assessing the work of a stronghold's artisans. They keep great volumes that describe various crafting techniques in detail, and use the guidelines in them to judge the quality of individual works.

The priests also evaluate young dwarves to determine the youths' vocations. The decisions of the priests are accepted without question.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Moradin learns the *mending* cantrip, and may cast *bless* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.


### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Volkanus/Lady Racca, the Planner\|Berronar Truesilver]]
Symbol: Intertwined silver rings
Represents: Home, Hearth, Law
Cleric Domains: Life, Order, Peace


The Matron of Home and Hearth is the patron of family, honor, and law. She lays out the rules for managing a dwarf clan.

Berronar's code establishes the laws of the dwarves, including contracts, trade agreements, and every other kind of bond forged through words and deeds. As Moradin provides the example that dwarves strive to match, Berronar provides the bonds that create dwarven society and culture.

Berronar's priests help unite couples and encourage marriages, to ensure that each generation of a clan is stronger and more talented than the last. This isn't mandatory, and a dwarf could disobey this recommendation; but so far none have ever had a reason to object.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Berronar learns the *guidance* cantrip, and may cast *sanctuary* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.

### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Thalassus of the Oceans\|Dumathoin]]
Symbol: A cut, faceted gem inside a mountain
Represents: The Secrets under the Mountain, the Deeps, Exploration.
Cleric Domains: Grave, Knowledge


The Lord of the Depths knows what is hidden and where to find it. He also knows that learning too much, too quickly, has a destabilising effect on people and communities. Dumathoin is the god who taught the Dwarves the process of [[Sounding\|Sounding]].

Priests of Dumathoin are the clan's record-keepers and often oversee mining exploration and townsite location activities. They also decide which mine tunnels will be used for interment, and consigning a dwarf's body to the depths of the mountain is the most sacred of their duties.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Dumathoin learns the *light* cantrip, and may cast *detect magic* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.

### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Maahes of Shield and Blade\|Clangeddin Silverbeard]]
Symbol: Crossed Battleaxes
Represents: War, Strategy
Cleric Domains: War


Known as the Father of Battle, Clangeddin Silverbeard is the patron of dwarf warriors. Impetuous and brave yet a cunning strategist, Clangeddin embodies the warrior's spirit that makes dwarven armies such formidable foes.

Clangeddin encourages dwarf warriors to venture out of the stronghold in search of foes to defeat. He particularly hates goblinoids, giants, and dragons. He compels his followers to seek out and dispatch such enemies before they can become a threat to the stronghold.

Clangeddin's faithful are mainly full-time warriors assigned to weapon training from an early age and expected to take the fight to the enemy. The two axes he wields embody his attitude, since he forsakes the added protection of a shield for the chance to deal more damage to his enemies.

Clangeddin's priests are warriors who lead from the front. When defending a stronghold, they guard the walls and lead sorties against enemy positions. When an external threat is near, the priests plan guerrilla raids to disrupt invaders before they can besiege the stronghold.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Clangeddin learns the *sacred flame* cantrip, and may cast *shield of faith* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.

# Minor Gods

### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of Volkanus/Dun, the Smelter\|Dun, the Smelter]]
Symbol: A crucible
Represents: Labour
Cleric Domains: Forge, Order, War

Known as the Smelter, the Crucible, or the Furnace master, Dun is Moradin's faithful worker, always following his father's wishes and preparing both the world and the dwarves as he has been told to do. Dun is a very literal being, with no creativity. He leaves the planning to others.

Dun is often the deity spending the most time with the Dwarven people, as his tasks are long and onerous. It is Dun who shows the master forge workers how to approach materials new to them, or methods they have not yet seen; he does this openly and intentionally, as he seems to know how to work with any material or process already. 

Those who follow Dun enter a life of service to their Clan; they are often thanked and celebrated, but their work is never finished. 

It is rare for a temple or shrine to Dun to exist independently of one to Moradin, for it is at Moradin's foundries and forges that Dun spends his time. Thus, Priests of Dun are most often found at those forges as well, following in the footsteps of their mentor.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Dun learns the *resistance* cantrip, and may cast *create or destroy water* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.

### [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Minor Deities/Aspects of The Earth Mother/The Winds/Estes, the Eastern Wind\|Hanseath]]
Symbol: A golden mug in front of a dark barrel. 
Represents: Ale, Celebration and Bounty
Cleric Domains: Life, Light, Peace

Your Favorite Uncle, as Hanseath is known, presides over celebration and revelry; but also gratefulness for the bounties that are received.

The teachings of Hanseath reinforce those of Berronar, in that properly appreciating the things you have, the friends and family around you, and the road ahead, make a community stronger.

Priests of Hanseath are often the Master brewers of a settlement, but are also put in charge of monuments, decorations, feasting days and any other communal events meant to spark joy- but also after funerals, losses in battle, or other tragedies, so as to remember what you had fondly.

#### Piety Benefit for Adherents
A dwarf who follows Hanseath learns the *thaumaturgy* cantrip, and may cast *purify food and drink* once per day, and using any spell slots they may have.
