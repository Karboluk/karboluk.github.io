# Synopsis
## Description

Aysu is a moth-like Fairy, with quick hands and pin-prick legs. Aysu is about 3'6, loves daggers, and family. She often wears a mask when working.

## Story
Aysu comes from a family notorious for being assassins for the Archfey [[Malice,\|Malice,]] leader of the Court of Shadows.  Her parents were sent on a mission to the material plane, and she snuck along behind them because she wanted to help/protect them - her parents only found her after they’d crossed through the portal.

As part of their contract was that a return portal wouldn’t appear until the job was finished, (insurance to make sure the job did get completed), her parents told her to stay put in a large manor while they went out to complete the contract. 

They never came back.

Too scared to go outside in an unfamiliar world, Aysu stayed at the manor for months, only leaving to find supplies. The house’s owner, who was a wealthy man who used the manor as a summer house, returned at one point; she hid from him for a few weeks, but he heard her skittering and fluttering around, and thought that his house was haunted.

He hired [[Adrastos\|Adrastos]] to help with the haunting, and Dras found a half-starved moth-child in the attic and brought her home.

  
### Before the game

Dras has been missing for 6 months, worrying the family and cutting off a source of income. [[Deryn\|Deryn]] and [[Iman\|Iman]] went out to look for him, leaving everyone else at home, still without enough money.

Aysu was deemed the best choice to go out because she usually looked relatively non-threatening (comparatively), and could always sneak away if things got too bad. The other family members couldn't leave for their own reasons, in any case.

Thus it was, in short, that Aysu was sent to work for [[Hamish Dougall\|Hamish Dougall]].

### Further developments

Aysu has since learned a few things. The Glasscatcher [[Helistica\|Helistica]] tracked her down, expecting to find her parents (372-09-02, in timeline 1). Helistica was not working for Malice, but for Mab, the Queen of Air and Darkness, checking on why the Archfey [[Sharatin\|Sharatin]], also known as Trevor the Quick, was still alive; this was the contract Aysu's parents were to fulfill. When they spoke, Helistica expressed her support, lent Aysu her shadow spear, and promised to come back when she could for more news, and more assistance as possible. She had not yet found Aysu again when the party began timeline 2.

Further, on 372-04-13 of timeline 2, Aysu has learned Sharatin was been the target of many such attempts, but has spared a great many of his assailants. [[Malice\|Malice]] sends those who she wishes to get rid of to him, but he either finds them sanctuary or sets them to work in the country of [[Lorica\|Lorica]], north of the Vale, if they still wish to fight for something.
