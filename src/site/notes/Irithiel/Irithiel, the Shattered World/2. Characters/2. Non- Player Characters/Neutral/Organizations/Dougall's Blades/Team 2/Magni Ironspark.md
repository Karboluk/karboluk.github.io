## Synopsis

Magni is a Dwarven Rogue, who favors daggers and crossbows. He is obviously greedy, not pleasant to look at, and grumpily quiet most of the time. It's known that he is heavily involved in the [[Kingfisher Blades\|Kingfisher Blades]], and has some level of leadership with them. He is also notably afraid of [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/2. Characters/2. Non- Player Characters/Neutral/Organizations/Dougall's Blades/Team 2/Etienne Deveraux\|Etienne Deveraux]]. 


### Last Seen 
Magni was last seen gathering marked coins before Team 3 defeated the Burning Man at the Ashen Candle Towers, on 372-08-20 in Timeline 1.
