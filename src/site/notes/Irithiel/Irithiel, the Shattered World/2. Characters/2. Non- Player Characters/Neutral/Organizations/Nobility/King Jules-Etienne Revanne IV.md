## Synopsis
The current King is a young man, 19 years of age. He became King 10 years ago when his father King Phillippe Revanne II died (no suspicions). He is smart, and perhaps will grow wise with time, but is known to lead with his heart and what he *hopes* will happen, rather than with concrete plans and sound policies.
