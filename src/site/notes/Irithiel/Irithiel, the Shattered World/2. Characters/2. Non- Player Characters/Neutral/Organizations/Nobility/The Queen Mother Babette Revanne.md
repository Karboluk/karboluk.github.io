## Synopsis
Widow of [[King Philippe II\|King Philippe II]], mother of [[Irithiel/ZZZ. Private DM Info/2. Characters/Non-Player Characters/VR372/Neutral/Nobility/Duchy Revanne/King Jules-Etienne Revanne IV-Master\|King Jules-Etienne Revanne IV-Master]], member of her Son's Privy Council. 

She is a noble woman, eldest child of the House of Albion's current Lord. She rose to power as anticipated, but has finished what could be assumed to be her duty quite early in life. 

The Queen Mother acted as Regent until her son grew of age, and was said to be much more intelligent and political than he is; she also steadfastly advocated for what was best for the populace, much to the consternation of the advisors of the time and their priorities. 

She now sits on [[King Revanne's Council\|King Revanne's Council]], though she misses more and more sessions as time goes by.
