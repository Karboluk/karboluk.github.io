## An Ancient Among Children
Gudvaer is by far the oldest member of the [[Njardarheimr Goliath\|Njardarheimr Goliath]], who live in the wastes east of the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/1. Shining Vale/1. Shining Vale General/The Shining Vale\|The Shining Vale]] circa VR-372.  Her old and hunched frame means she walks with a pronounced limp, but her pride means she will refuse any overt assistance. 

Gudvaer is clearly the power behind this tribe, and any choice she makes is enacted. It is her guidance that chooses the tribe's path, her words that shape their beliefs, and her anger that cows any unacceptable disorder.

The members of the Njardarheimr tribe keep her presence a secret when possible, which is something she has trained them to do.

# Hidden

Gudvaer is the current holder of the Giant's mark in the [[Irithiel/ZZZ. Private DM Info/49. Secrets/Prophecy and Foretelling/Tryptic Prophecy\|Tryptic Prophecy]], and as with the Elves, she is given deference in every matter.

- 890 year old female, Stone Goliath. She was a little over 7 feet tall, but her old and hunched frame means she seems shorter.
- Dreadlocked black and grey hair.
- Missing her right eye, with two long vertical scars marring that side of her face.
	- She has never explained how this happened. The current tribe members know not to ask her. 
- She walks with a pronounced limp, but her pride means she will refuse any assistance. 
- She has taught all of the Lorekeepers of the tribe for as long as anyone can remember
- She eats only raw meat, pulling sinew from bone with hands and teeth.
- Gudvaer was the tribe's Lorekeeper- or  Sagnavörður (Sag-na-verther), which in those days was more of a shamanistic role also incorporating that of the navigator. 
	- This is how she knew of the prophecy, and how she heard the call of Damu before gaining the mark. 
- Gudvaer's eye was lost after a battle with Jarðskjálftavaldur (jarth-skyoltavaltur, meaning Earthshaker), the Cloud Giant who was the previous holder of the Giant's Mark. 
	- The Giant's Mark must be taken, it does not pass otherwise.	
- She took the mark from him 870 years ago, slaying him in on the north continent.
- Gudvaer follows the guidance of the Earth Mother in the form of the expansion of the Trackless Clay- where it goes, her tribe goes. They scout the edges of the clay often to see where it has expanded to, and she is concerned that they can not go past the hobgoblins.



When Gudvaer dies, unless it is because someone takes the Mark, the [[Irithiel/ZZZ. Private DM Info/49. Secrets/Prophecy and Foretelling/Tryptic Prophecy\|Tryptic Prophecy]] will be broken.
