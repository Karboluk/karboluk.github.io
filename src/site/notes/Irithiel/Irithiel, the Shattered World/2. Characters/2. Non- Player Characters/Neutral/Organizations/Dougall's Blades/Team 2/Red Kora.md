## Synopsis
Red Kora is a Fire Goliath Ranger, who prefers to travel with a beast companion. She is aggressive and driven, but quite friendly if you are going her way. She left Team 2 when they decided to abandon their job hunting the Blue Dragon Siskrimit; not out of honour, but what she perceived in them as greedy cowardice. 

Red Kora said that she was ostracised by her tribe in the Varak range, who are all Stone Goliath, for her heritage. She wants to return with glorious accomplishments to prove herself to them.


### Last Seen
Red Kora was last seen after the fight with Siskrimit, where her Hook Horror companion Klee-Kee was disintegrated on 372-06-12 in timeline 1. She was dejected, and mumbled something about going off to find a new companion before returning to adventuring. She headed north, further into the mountain range.
