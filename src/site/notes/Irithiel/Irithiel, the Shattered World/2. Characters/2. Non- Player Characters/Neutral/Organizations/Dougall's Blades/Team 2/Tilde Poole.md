## Synopsis

Tilde is a luxury-coveting attention-seeking lower-class wannabe with a Corwyn accent she tries to hide, who seems to be hanging around for fun; not that you'd know that with the amount of complaining she does. Assuming you are deaf, you still know that she's near if you smell too much cheap perfume.

Tilde loves to order everyone on Team 2 around, except Etienne. She treats him like a fragile egg of a man, perhaps to escape his temper. She uses her magic to confuse, charm and control.

### Last Seen
Tilde was last seen gathering marked coins before Team 3 defeated the Burning Man at the Ashen Candle Towers, on 372-08-20 in Timeline 1.
