# Situation

After a day of preparation- weapons being passed around, a trebuchet constructed, watches kept, the encampment of the Njardarheimr Goliath has moved from an excited state to a tense but flat wait. 

As the sun begins to set against the just-barely-visible peaks to the west, with a sudden and jarring ***CRACK***; a hobgoblin army simply comes into view. Teleportation. 
- (*note: a teleportation accuracy was rolled, and was high. They could have appeared in a bad state, or some mangled by mishap, but the dice went their way this time.*)

They are hard to look at with the sun immediately behind then, but they are clearly prepared. Someone has been watching this place from afar and the troops are already in formation. This was a well planned and executed attack, at a chosen time, to give them an advantage from the start. Shield walls go up before anyone could possibly close, a gapped line of steel in a slightly arced line facing the encampment. From behind that wall, a rain of arrows starts buzzing through the air and pelting the dragon wing canopy above like hail on thick wood. It assaults your ears but does no damage to the canopy or the Goliath; the first good news- they didn't know how durable this place is, their information is incomplete.

The Goliath brandish their tall bone war horns. A loud and harsh note blares forth, sounding like the Hulkar, from the mouth of the horns. With a furious cry the giant-kin begin to rush their foe.

Esmond's yell, part of the plan, is drowned out by the Goliath, but has no special effect.

# The Battle Method
## System/Rules

### Turns
Combat is done in 10 minute Phases. Simple things can happen just by being declared- skill checks, healing etc. Spells or abilities with durations between 1 minute and 10 minutes are considered to be active for the entire Phase, for simplicity, unless they have a limited duration to gate damage/healing (*aura of vitality*, for example), in which case they have their effect and other spell can also be cast.

Moving between the *Canopy* or the *Front Line* to the *Back Line*, and vice versa, takes 1 phase, as you are being harried the whole time by suppressing fire and/or magic and/or blocked by Infantry and must go wide. Once one of the Front or Back lines is gone, this cost no longer applies.

### Objectives
There are 7 objectives which decide which army will win. The first to 4 decides the victory for the armies.
- It is possible to win the army battle, and lose the overall battle, and conversely lose the army battle and win the overall battle. An example of this would be losing the army battle, but escaping with those you care about and can save.
- More than 4 losses results in the death of named combatants on your side, as can certain multi-phase objectives lasting for specific durations.
- More than 4 victories will give you bonuses as you go and potentially assistance in the final confrontation. 

Any objective *can* be handled with traditional combat, though attacking into an army using DnD 5e is ill-advised. Objectives can also be handled with spells, skills, actions, and plans; be creative about how you would go about resolving a given situation. Breaking concentration, dispelling magic, mitigating damage, sabotage, tactical assassination, and the list goes on.

Some objectives will result in traditional combat, even if you attempt to resolve it with other means, as resolving some objectives like ***The Siegecraft*** will liberate the soldiers in that unit, who will then be free to attack you- and motivated to do so.

Passing within range of any unit causes damage to your characters- even if attention is focused elsewhere, some soldiers will harry you or try to engage. The unit's range is dependent on their composition, and a Dexterity saving throw against the Martial DC can reduce it by 1/2.

Multiple objectives can be attempted on the same Phase if the party splits, but if an objective attempted by a smaller squad results in combat, this will be more difficult than if the entire party is present. 


### Difficulty
There are two separate DCs, for different types of actions. 
- The ***Martial DC*** is applied to any physical attack against, skill checks to cause physical damage to, or attempts to confuse or maneuver enemy units without using magic, etc. it is also used to try to 1/2 any damage taken by passing within range of a unit and being subject to physical attacks. 
- The ***Magical DC*** is applied to any attempt to dispel or mitigate enemy magic; to harm, inflict conditions, or maneuver enemy units; and to resist against any magic that those units may be using on you.

The Martial and Magical DCs begin at a predetermined number and are affected by:
- The condition and current circumstance of the unit in question.
- The actions of the Party during an objective to help accomplish their chosen goal.
	- Spells to blind, confuse, slow
	- Attempts to break concentration
	- Physical harrying such as Glory's Trebuchet
