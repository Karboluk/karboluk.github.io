The Luxan Empire existed in the distant past in the place now partially occupied by [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/1. Shining Vale/1. Shining Vale General/The Shining Vale\|The Shining Vale]], [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/2. Others/2. Subdurni/Subdurni\|Subdurni]], [[Lorica\|Lorica]], and [[The High Depths\|The High Depths]], having fallen long before the elves of [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/1. Shining Vale/2. Duchy Revanne/The Enclave\|The Enclave]] or the [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/4. Geography, Lands and Countries/1. The Continent of the Shining Vale/2. Others/Sea Kingdom of Kilar\|Sea Kingdom of Kilar]] arrived. Their sites are clearly constructed on intersections of lines of power. From the archeological evidence, they appear to have been mostly Tabaxi, with with a sizable slave-caste of dwarves.

It seems clear that the Luxans worshiped [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Helios the Radiant Truth\|Helios]] primarily, and evidence of worship of later gods is absent from their temples- most notably [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Volkanus]], or more accurately Moradin, as dwarven construction would normally carry. 

This notable absence asks a much more interesting question, which seems to have two equally interesting possible answers: Either this indicates the presence of Dwarves prior to [[Irithiel/Irithiel, the Shattered World/5. Religions and Deities/Deities/Major Deities/Volkanus the Maker and Reshaper of all Things\|Volkanus]]'s emergence in [[Thracia\|Thracia]], which is problematic as he is commonly regarded as the Dwarven creator god, or somehow the normal dwarven cultural tendencies were erased or suppressed during this time; not an easy feat.
